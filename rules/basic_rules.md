# 1. Object of the game
Each player has his own goal, depending on his role card:
    + Sheriff: must eliminate all Outlaws and the Renegade(s);
    + Outlaws: must eliminate the Sheriff;
    + Deputies: must help and protect the Sheriff;
    + Renegade: must be the last character in play.

# 2. Preparation
1. Take as many roles as the number of players, divided as follows:
    1. 4 players: 1 Sheriff, 1 Renegade, 2 Outlaws;
    2. 5 players: 1 Sheriff, 1 Renegade, 2 Outlaws, 1 Deputy;
    3. 6 players: 1 Sheriff, 1 Renegade, 3 Outlaws, 1 Deputy;
    4. 7 players: 1 Sheriff, 1 Renegade, 3 Outlaws, 2 Deputies;
    5. 8 players: 1 Sheriff, 2 Renegades, 3 Outlaws, 2 Deputies.
Shuffle the roles and deal one, face down, to each player.

2. The Sheriff reveals himself by turning his card face up. All other
players look at their role but must keep it secret.

3. Shuffle the characters and give one face up to each player. Each
player now announces the name of his character and reads his ability
aloud.

4. Each player takes a number of bullets as shown on his character
card (1). The Sheriff plays the game with two additional bullets (2)

5. The Sheriff takes the five dice and starts the game.

# 3. The Game
The game is played in turns, in clockwise order. On your turn, you will:
1. Roll all five dice
2. You may then choose to keep the dice or re-roll some or all of them up to
    two times. If you roll a third time, you may also re-roll any dice you didn’t
    choose to re-roll on your second roll. You must accept the third roll.
3. When you are satisfied with your dice roll (or you’re out of re-rolls),
    resolve the dice results.
4. Your turn is over and play passes to the player on your left.

# 4. The Dice
The dice show six different symbols. You must apply all the dice results in the
following order. You cannot pass on a rolled die: you must use them all (1)

1. Indian arrow: You must resolve this die immediately when
    rolled, not at the end of your turn. Take one arrow token (one per
    arrow rolled) and keep it in front of you. You may roll this die again
    if you have re-rolls left. If you take the last arrow, the Indians attack and
    each player loses one life point for each arrow in front of them. After the
    attack, all players discard their arrows, and you resume your turn.

2. Dynamite: This die cannot be re-rolled (1). If you roll three or
    more Dynamites, you cannot reroll anymore dice this turn (2), and
    you lose one life point (3). All your other dice results are then resolved
    as normal unless you are eliminated by the Dynamite(4)

3. Bull’s Eye “1”: Choose the player next to you on your left or
    right. That player loses one life point.

4. Bull’s Eye “2”: Choose the player exactly two places to your
    left or right (1). That player loses one life point. If there are only two
    or three players left in the game, treat this as Bull’s Eye “1” (2).
    Bull’s Eye “1” and Bull’s Eye “2” are first assigned, then they are all resolved 
    at the same time (3). You can assign more results to a player than the number of
    life points they have (4). When counting places, do not include players who
    were eliminated on a previous turn (5).

4. Beer: Choose any player: he gains one life point (1). You may choose
    yourself (2). You can never have more life points than the number
    you had at the beginning of the game (3). If you choose a player with
    his maximum life points, this result is wasted (4).

5. Gatling: If you roll three or more Gatlings you activate the
    Gatling Gun, and each of the other players loses one life point (1).
    Also, you discard all of your arrows (2).

# 5. Running Out of Life Points—A Player Is Eliminated
If you lose your last life point, you are out of the game (1). Show your role to
all players (2) and discard all your arrows (3). If you are eliminated, you do not
act in the game anymore. But, if your team partners win, you win too (3).

# 6. End of the Game
The game ends immediately if:
1. The Sheriff is eliminated: If a Renegade is the only one alive, he
    wins. Otherwise, all Outlaws win as a team.
2. All the Outlaws and Renegades are eliminated: The Sheriff and all
    Deputies win as a team.
Note: In an 8-player game, each of the two Renegades plays on their own,
and wins only if he is the last player alive (1). If, in the final stages of the
game, the Sheriff is confronted by two Renegades and the Sheriff is
eliminated first, the Outlaws win (2)!
